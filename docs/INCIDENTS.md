Crowd crushes build for minutes, in known places, before people die. Johansson, Helbing et al. (2008) analysed video of the 2006 Mina (Hajj) disaster. They found that stop-and-go waves and then "crowd turbulence" appeared about 10 minutes before the accident. Their crowd-pressure measure, local density × variance of velocity, is the core of CrowdShield's warning.

Reference: A. Johansson, D. Helbing, H. Z. Al-Abideen, S. Al-Bosta, "From crowd dynamics to crowd safety: a video-based analysis", Advances in Complex Systems 11(4), 497–527 (2008).
